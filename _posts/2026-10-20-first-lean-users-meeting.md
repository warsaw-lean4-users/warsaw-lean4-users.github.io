We are starting a series of Lean users meetings, which we hope to hold monthly.
Our first meeting is on Tuesday, 27 October.

- **When:** Tuesday, 27 October 2026, at 6pm (Warsaw time).
- **Where:** Room 4420.

We will review the most active topics in the Lean 4 community over the summer,
as well as upcoming events and opportunities for further involvement. The talk
will also briefly cover the current state of software verification and funding
opportunities for research in this area.

[Presentation materials](https://github.com/warsaw-lean4-users/meetings)
