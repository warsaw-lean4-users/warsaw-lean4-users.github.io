MIMUW, the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics at the University
of Warsaw, has a new course: **Dowodzenie twierdzeń w języku Lean**
(*Theorem proving in Lean*). **Marcin Pilipczuk** and **Rafał Stefański** are
teaching it in the **winter semester of 2026/27**.

The course introduces formalizing mathematics in **Lean 4** and using
**Mathlib**. Topics range from functional programming and dependent types to
inductive constructions, monads, the correspondence between proofs and
programs, and proof tactics.

## Course at a glance

- **Language:** Polish.
- **Credits:** 6 ECTS.
- **Format:** 30 hours of lectures and 30 hours of lab sessions.
- **Prerequisite:** Functional Programming (*Programowanie funkcyjne*).
  The listing also names Lambda Calculus and Advanced Functional Programming
  as assumed background.
- **Assessment:** a presentation near the end of the semester and a final project.
- **Course code:** 1000-2M26DTL.
