A team from **Adam Mickiewicz University (UAM)** and **Warsaw University of
Technology (PW)** has received a Renaissance Philanthropy grant for a project
on agentic computer algebra systems, as part of its AI4Math work.

The project aims to build an open-source system connecting large language models,
computer algebra systems, and Lean proof assistants. It will let an AI agent run
calculations in tools such as Magma, SageMath, and PARI/GP, check results using
an independent system, and produce a machine-checked Lean proof for the result.

The team is led by **Bartosz Naskręcki** (principal investigator, UAM and PW)
and includes **Patryk Żywica** (UAM), **Jan Piotrowski** (PW), and **Gustaw
Wenzel** (PW).
